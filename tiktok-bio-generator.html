What Makes a Great TikTok Bio?

TikTok bios are limited to 80 characters — less than a tweet. In that tiny space, you need to tell people who you are, what you make, and why they should follow. The best TikTok bios are punchy, specific, and show personality in every word.

TikTok Bio Tips

  • Lead with your content type — tell people exactly what they'll see if they follow you.
  • Use your posting schedule — "New video every day" or "Every Friday" gives people a reason to follow rather than just like and scroll.
  • Show personality — TikTok rewards authenticity. A funny, self-aware line beats a formal description every time.
  • End with a CTA — "Link in bio," "DM me," or "Follow for daily X" tells people what to do next.
  • Use line breaks — breaking your bio into 3-4 short lines makes it much easier to read on mobile.

TikTok Bio Ideas by Niche

For Comedy Creators

Lead with a self-aware joke about your content. "Making videos my mum thinks are embarrassing" is more compelling than "Comedy creator | Daily content." Specificity and self-awareness are your biggest assets.

For Educational Creators

State exactly what you teach and who it's for. "Explaining finance in plain English | New video every Monday" tells potential followers exactly what they're signing up for — and sets expectations you can meet.

For Lifestyle Creators

Paint a picture of your aesthetic in a few words. Location, vibe, and one defining interest work well: "🌿 Slow living in the countryside | Recipes, interiors & honest life."

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a TikTok bio be?

TikTok bios are limited to 80 characters. This is much shorter than Instagram or Twitter, which is why every word needs to count.

Should I use emojis in my TikTok bio?

Yes — emojis are essential on TikTok. They act as visual bullet points, add personality, and make your bio easier to scan on a small screen. Use 2-4 emojis maximum.

What should a TikTok bio say?

Your TikTok bio should say: what content you make, how often you post, and what makes you different or worth following. Keep it fun, specific, and under 80 characters.
