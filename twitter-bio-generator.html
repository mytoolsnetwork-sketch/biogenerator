How to Write a Great Twitter/X Bio

Your Twitter bio has 160 characters to tell the world who you are and why they should follow you. The best Twitter bios are specific, personality-driven, and give a clear sense of what kind of content you share. Generic bios ("Lover of life | Coffee addict | Dog mom") blend into the background — specific bios stand out.

Twitter Bio Formula That Works

The most effective Twitter bios follow a simple structure: Role + Topics + Personality touch + Optional extra (location, employer, disclaimer). For example: "UX designer at @Figma | Tweets about design systems, accessibility & the occasional hot take | Based in Amsterdam."

Twitter Bio Tips

  • Be specific about your topics — "I tweet about AI" is better than "Tech enthusiast." "AI policy & regulation" is better still.
  • Show personality — a self-aware joke, an unexpected detail, or a dry observation makes your bio memorable.
  • Use "opinions my own" — if you work for a company but tweet independently, this disclaimer signals authenticity.
  • Include your location — it helps people find like-minded local connections and adds context to your perspective.
  • Don't waste characters on adjectives — "passionate," "enthusiastic," and "lover of" add nothing. Replace them with specifics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a Twitter bio be?

Twitter/X bios are limited to 160 characters — slightly longer than Instagram but still very short. Every word needs to earn its place.

What should I put in my Twitter bio?

Include: your role or what you tweet about, a personality touch, and optionally your location or employer. Avoid vague adjectives — specificity is everything on Twitter.

Should I use hashtags in my Twitter bio?

Generally no — hashtags in bios used to help with discovery but are less effective now. Use the space for personality and specifics instead.
