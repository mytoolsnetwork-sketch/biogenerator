About MyBioMaker

Writing a bio sounds simple — until you're staring at an empty profile field trying to sum up who you are in two lines. MyBioMaker was built to make that moment painless.

Our free generator creates personalized bios for every major platform — Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter/X, and general professional bios — tailored to each platform's character limits, tone, and audience expectations. No sign-up required, just instant results you can copy and use right away.

🎯 Our Mission

To help anyone — creators, professionals, businesses, and everyday users — put their best foot forward online without spending an hour second-guessing every word.

📱 Built for Every Platform

Each platform has its own rules: Instagram gives you 150 characters, Twitter/X gives you 160, TikTok just 80. Our generator respects these limits and adapts tone and structure to match what works best on each platform.

🛠️ Dedicated Bio Generators

Beyond our main tool, we've built platform-specific generators: Instagram bio generator, LinkedIn bio generator, TikTok bio generator, Twitter/X bio generator, and a professional bio generator for websites and press kits.

MyBioMaker is independently built and maintained. We don't require any personal information to use our tools, and we never sell your data. Have feedback or a platform you'd like us to add? We'd love to hear from you.
